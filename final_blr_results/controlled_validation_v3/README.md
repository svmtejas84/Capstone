# Controlled validation v3

The isolated, authorized Bengaluru campaign is complete. Begin with `FINAL_SCIENTIFIC_VERDICT.md`, `REPRODUCIBILITY.md`, and `verification/FINAL_VERIFICATION.md`. `PROTOCOL_FREEZE.md` is the pre-fit plan; `PROTOCOL_AMENDMENT_01.md` and `PROTOCOL_AMENDMENT_02.md` document corrections made before test scoring. The original v2m array failed the full future-mutation gate and its attempted fits are quarantined. The repaired array passed the gate, but operational weather-release/ingestion times remain unverified.

All five horizons, five seeds, five matched regularizer conditions and six same-information neural baseline families were completed. The one-time test evaluation is **exploratory** because this Bengaluru interval had previously been examined. It does not establish an independent temporal or spatial replication. No second-city or manuscript work was performed.

- `EXPERIMENT_REGISTER.csv`: every completed valid and quarantined fit cell.
- `data_manifests/`: source hashes, repaired array and per-station eligibility.
- `training/`: validation-selected checkpoints and epoch histories, including quarantined attempts.
- `predictions/`: lossless observed-pair predictions, targets and indices.
- `metrics/`, `statistical_analysis/`: full forecasting, regularizer, seed, station and coverage results.
- `figures/`: six PNG/PDF/SVG result figures. A new isolated-station F7 is explicitly not evaluated.
- `logs/`, `RESUME.md`: supervised execution and exact recovery state.
- `archives/`: local checksum-verified export; selected checkpoints are included, restart-only partial checkpoint tensors remain listed by local hash.

Historical experiments and publication assets were not modified.
