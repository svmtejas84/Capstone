5. **Existing neural comparisons:** all 35 requested paired contrasts are VERIFIED on matched 14,583 pairs per horizon using five-seed mean predictions and 2,000 UTC-day bootstrap draws (`baselines/paired_architecture_comparisons.csv`). Original ST-PIGNN outperforms Vanilla GCN, GRU-only and ST GRU-GCN at h=3,6,12 by nominal CI and exploratory Holm; at h=1 it outperforms Vanilla GCN and ST GRU-GCN, but has higher MAE than GRU-only. No h=24 neural comparison survives Holm. This is retrospective evidence on a repeatedly exposed test set, not a new confirmatory win.
6. **Multiplicity:** all 35 architecture contrasts form one exploratory Holm family with paired UTC-day sign-flip p values. Twenty-one adjusted tests reject at 0.05; the exact signed results are in the CSV. Historical nominal intervals and fixed horizon win/physics criteria were not changed. `results/benchmark_v2j.md` and `results/lambda_check_v2j.csv` retain the original decisions: h=1 lambda=0.06 helps under its fixed paired rule, h=24 does not; corrected LOSO has no neural WIN and corrected DEM meets no all-control criterion.

Standalone two-layer MLP and temporal CNN would require new training if the supervisor wants those comparisons. `baselines/baseline_training_proposal.md` fixes prospective configuration, seeds, GPU/storage estimate and limitations. v2k did not start them.

Details: `road_graph/road_graph_integrity.md` and `graph_measurements_v2k.json`.
