# Minimum station-to-road bridge

Path B is necessary for any future forecast because the original road-partition model's current feature construction is not cutoff safe. `spatial_decoder.py` supplies a fixed p=2 inverse-distance forecast propagation baseline and a compact **1,249-parameter** residual MLP. It takes only eligible support-station *forecasts at a common forecast origin*, UTM coordinates and verified SRTM elevation. It returns road estimates plus nearest-support distance. Its final layer starts at zero, so the untrained output equals IDW exactly. No OSM edge is interpreted as atmospheric flow. The bridge does not fabricate road labels or use the target-hour monitor observations.

The code is a feasibility component, **not a fitted estimator**. `forecast_idw` is directly runnable with verified cutoff-safe station forecasts; `CompactSpatialDecoder` requires spatially isolated training targets. The caller must verify forecast cutoff, support-fold provenance, station isolation and train-fold-only preprocessing. Fixed normalization constants in the prototype are 100 µg/m³, 10 km, 15 stations and 100 m elevation difference; these are implementation scales, not calibrated uncertainty or selected predictive hyperparameters.

For a genuine W6 LOSO comparison, each target fold needs the support stations' h=6 forecasts generated by a model fit that excluded the target station's history and labels. These *cross-fitted support forecasts* are not archived. Predictions saved from separate held-out-support folds cannot be pooled: their models may have trained on the target station. The archived corrected W6 reports contain aggregate comparison results, not the necessary per-origin, per-support forecast tensor or fold checkpoints. Therefore no empirical decoder fitting or station-level validation was run in v2q. A pilot requires generating the eligible forecasts under the frozen protocol; doing so across all 15 folds is a new comparative training campaign and needs a measured budget/approval beyond this feasibility round.

The eventual exact matched comparison is training-only target-station mean, station forecasts propagated by p=2 IDW, and the same forecasts plus compact decoder. All three must predict the same future observation masks. An optional validation-only pilot may use one fold and seed 42, maximum ten epochs, with no test data; it would measure engineering feasibility only, not spatial generalization. A city map is gated on stronger paired LOSO evidence and complete provenance.
