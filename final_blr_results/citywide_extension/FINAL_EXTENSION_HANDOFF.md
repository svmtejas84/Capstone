# Bengaluru v2o/v2p research extension handoff

**Both authorized phases reached terminal states.** v2o produced explicit retrospective IDW road-node estimates; v2p implemented and tested a falsifiable reliability-gated loss but made no predictive performance claim. No paper, new road-graph neural model, unrestricted GPU campaign or external-city training was performed. Earlier v2j–v2n artifacts and three older ZIP archives remain untouched.

## v2o

1. **Full-city execution:** yes, three prespecified UTC snapshots over all **154,902** OSM road nodes, giving **464,706** complete finite estimate rows. This was CPU IDW, not corrected ST-PIGNN inference. Total supervised job time 10.14 s, peak process RSS about 418 MiB; full IDW kernel 0.081–0.165 s per snapshot. No GPU VRAM was used. Source/output checks and maps are in `v2o/CITYWIDE_EXECUTION_RESULTS.md` and `v2o/`.
2. **Method and label:** fixed inverse-distance-squared interpolation from 4/6/4 *same-hour observed monitors*. Values are **retrospective spatial estimates**, neither observed road concentrations nor lead-time neural forecasts. The corrected ST-PIGNN still lacks matching weights and honest road-node histories/anchors.
3. **Spatial validation:** genuine held-out station exclusion was applied. Eleven stations were scored, four remain without test observations. IDW covered 12,328/14,583 observed pairs (84.54%) with pooled MAE 23.133 µg/m³; on the same IDW-eligible pairs nearest MAE was 27.007 and a training-only other-station mean 21.943. IDW was weaker than the simple mean, and the exposed Bengaluru test cannot independently confirm spatial generalization. The earlier W6 ST-PIGNN LOSS to ridge remains unchanged.
4. **Unsensed roads:** 84.9–90.6% of selected nodes are outside the eligible-monitor hull or >5 km from the closest monitor. Maps gray them out. Distance/hull are **uncalibrated support diagnostics**, not prediction intervals. No independent road sensor truth or citywide issue-time differentiated wind exists. The road graph's structural validity and full IDW computation do not establish street-level accuracy, route exposure gain or ST-PIGNN-specific city inference.

v2o separate verdicts for structural integrity, feature completeness, estimator validity, computational execution, independent generalization, forecasting, uncertainty and ST-PIGNN inference are in `v2o/CITYWIDE_INFERENCE_VERDICT.md`. The local v2o ZIP is `v2o/research_results_v2o.zip`, 18,997,765 bytes, SHA-256 `cec02a61e38708c96326469ffa65786fae6acfa5dd0e422f05afae7762637bf8`, 32 members verified.

## v2p RGDR

5. **Implementation and tests:** `src/stpignn/rgdr.py` implements binary cutoff-observation gating of the original directed hinge and a reliability-weighted non-directional smoothing comparator. The all-one gate equals the existing fixed penalty; eight focused physics/RGDR tests passed. No architecture redesign or historical output edit occurred.
6. **Novelty:** **NOT ESTABLISHED.** Missingness-aware time-series models, adaptive physics-loss weighting and wind-aware sparse-sensor graph air-quality models are already in the primary-source prior-art table `v2p_rgdr/RGDR_PRIOR_ART_REVIEW.md`. An application-specific controlled comparison remains a hypothesis.
7. **Training/validation diagnostics:** no neural training or validation forecast score was produced. The fixed binary gate retains only **2.27–2.36%** of original eligible directional training edge instances and leaves about **79–80%** of training target hours with zero gated edges; validation retention is 6.66–6.75%. This is a negative feasibility signal. CUDA was unavailable in the sandbox, and no CPU model fit was substituted. The result does not justify test-driven gate relaxation or a positive RGDR claim.
8. **Evidence needed:** an eligible independently held-out dataset, a source-quality/forecast-cutoff audit, matched λ=0/fixed-directional/reliability-weighted-smoothing/RGDR fits, train-only effective-penalty scale control, multi-seed and spatial-holdout evaluation, uncertainty/multiplicity accounting, and external replication. The prospective frozen design is `v2p_rgdr/RGDR_FROZEN_EVALUATION_PROTOCOL.md`.
9. **Supervisor approval still required:** the independent dataset and backup destination, a measured CUDA preflight/pilot budget, any binary-gate revision, and the proposed 100-final-fit/80-selection-fit comparative campaign. No second-city training is automatically authorized.
