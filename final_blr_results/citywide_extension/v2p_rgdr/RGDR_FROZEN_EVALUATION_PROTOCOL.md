# Prospective RGDR evaluation protocol for supervisor review

**Prepared, not authorized for full execution.** No independent eligible dataset is presently verified. This protocol cannot turn Bengaluru's repeatedly examined test set into confirmation. Any future campaign requires supervisor approval of dataset eligibility, source provenance, GPU budget and fixed controls before launch.

Eligibility gate: an external city must have at least 30 reference-grade, hourly PM2.5 monitoring stations with at least two years of verifiable aligned observations, reliable issue-time meteorology and documented quality flags. The earlier London and Copenhagen eligibility audits did not satisfy/verify that gate; no second-city training is authorized. Hold out spatially distinct stations and a later untouched temporal block, with no held-out pollutant channels entering features, imputation, reliability, scalers, calibration or selection.

Freeze before any external test: exact binary cutoff-observation reliability `r=(1−m_src)(1−m_dst)`; wind mask and resistance from `src/stpignn/physics_v2.py`; 15-station corrected architecture only if that graph/data protocol is eligible, otherwise define a new matched architecture protocol first. Controls are λ=0, fixed directional, reliability-weighted generic smoothing and RGDR. Use global λ=0.06 with the original epoch warm-up for the two directional variants, five seeds [11,23,37,51,79], horizons [1,3,6,12,24], width 64, batch 512, max 30 epochs, patience 5, AdamW and LR grid [1e−4,3e−4,1e−3,3e−3], identical training/validation selection opportunities. Check and log actual effective penalty and gradient magnitudes on a fixed train-only batch set; the generic control's scale is the ratio of fixed-directional to generic initial train-only median gradient norms, fixed before validation, or the comparison is reported unscaled with that confound explicit. If the ratio denominator is zero or unstable, stop rather than choose a test-favorable scale.

Primary outcome: observed-target MAE at each horizon, plus RMSE and R². Primary contrasts are RGDR minus fixed, RGDR minus λ=0 and RGDR minus weighted generic at each of five horizons: one 15-comparison family. Use matched station/time targets, 2,000 paired UTC-day bootstrap replicates and 2,000 day-level sign-flip replicates with Holm adjustment. Report all seeds and ensemble separately, per-station errors/coverage, zero-gate exposure, seven-day block sensitivity and missingness strata. A benefit requires lower MAE with adjusted p<0.05 and interval in the favorable direction, with no material degradation of spatial holdout/coverage. Independent external-site replication is required for a publishable general claim. Bengaluru test results, if ever examined after freeze, are exploratory only and cannot tune this protocol.

Resource preflight: 4 controls × 5 horizons × 5 seeds = 100 final fits, plus 4 LR candidates × 4 controls × 5 horizons with seed 11 = up to 80 selection fits; cap total at 180 fits. Prior 32-GiB V100 station experiments suggest feasibility but do not establish this campaign's memory/time. Require a one-batch CUDA memory check, supervised locking/restartable per-cell checkpoints, a measured pilot runtime, ≤24-hour approved GPU budget, and an independent backup destination before the full run. Stop on unavailable CUDA, missing source features, nonfinite gradients, insufficient external station count, leakage, unstable generic scale, or budget overrun. Do not replace a blocked dataset with fabricated road labels.
