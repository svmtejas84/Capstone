# Directional penalty: exposure, violations and gradient replay

## Definitions and distinct populations

The training loss uses **all 15 station predictions** on issue-hour directed arcs selected by the actual or control wind mask. For every active arc source i→destination j, `src/stpignn/physics_v2.py:batched_penalty` applies the squared positive part of `(pred_j−pred_i)/training_target_SD × sigmoid(edge_length_km)` and divides by the number of active arc-hours. The V3 lambda schedule reaches 0.06 at epoch 5; all final selected checkpoints are at epoch ≥10. This is a directional concentration-ordering heuristic, not a mass balance. There is no calm-wind speed cutoff; finite directions can remain mask-active when speed is zero. The shared wind reference is the archive series used at station index 0, at issue row c=T−h.

At six hours, the true-wind mask has 185,890 active directed arc-hours across 13,382 training target-hours, averaging **13.89 active arcs per selected hour**, or 23.15% of 60 arcs. V3 true-wind training epochs recorded zero batches with no active directional arc. The V4 full-calendar shuffle has 189,514 active arcs on selected training issue hours; reverse wind 190,617; constant north 120,438. The invalidated pre-amendment shuffle count 185,890 must not be used. These densities differ, so equal lambda does not imply equal total optimization exposure. The corrected full shuffle preserves the 433,416 active mask entries over *all* calendar training hours and does not use target availability to construct its permutation.

The **post-hoc test diagnostic** is narrower: actual-wind arcs whose source and destination *targets* are both observed, with threshold 0.05 in standardized physical-target units (0.05×22.993734=1.149687 µg/m³). At h6 it has 5,817 eligible arc-hours; 2,112 of 4,063 target hours have no eligible observed-endpoint arc. This denominator differs from training and cannot prove atmospheric compliance. `PENALTY_DIAGNOSTICS_V3.csv` retains all five horizons × five original controls × four thresholds, copied from frozen V3. `PENALTY_DIAGNOSTICS_V4.csv` recomputes the common h6 diagnostic for V3 zero/true/original Laplacian and all six new V4 controls.

At h6 and threshold 0.05, V3 true wind has **1,453/5,817 = 24.979%** ordering violations; V3 λ=0 has **1,431/5,817 = 24.600%**. The true-wind model does **not** lower violation frequency against zero under this diagnostic. On the same eligible arc population, mean squared positive normalized difference is 0.2383 for true wind versus 0.2934 for zero: the heuristic may reduce violation magnitude while crossing the threshold slightly more often. Stronger true-wind lambda 0.3 and 1.0 give frequencies 25.56% and 25.73%, but mean squared positive differences 0.1885 and 0.1302; their test MAEs worsen. This supports a penalty-magnitude effect, not a predictive or physical-validity advantage. The original Laplacian frequency is 25.34% and squared magnitude 0.2161. The post-hoc threshold and observed-endpoint restriction are explicit in `scripts/analyze_sensitivity.py` and V3 `scripts/wind_cone_diagnostics.py`.

## Frozen-batch gradient measurement

`PENALTY_GRADIENT_REPLAY.csv` measures the first **512 training target-hours** for each saved seed-11 selected checkpoint, with no optimizer step. The script verifies each checkpoint and array SHA-256, loads the exact historical model, constructs observed-target supervised loss and raw regularizer loss, and calls autograd separately. cuDNN requires the GRU module to be in training mode for backward; the backbone has no dropout, and no parameter update occurs. The first replay attempt failed in eval mode; its supervisor log is preserved, then the diagnostic was corrected and passed. The replay is a single batch and seed, not a complete training trajectory.

At h6 on the V3 true-wind checkpoint: raw supervised loss **0.463506**, raw directional loss **0.145391**, weighted directional loss **0.008723** at λ=0.06. Supervised gradient norm is **2.95758**, weighted-penalty norm **0.117736**, ratio **0.03981**, and gradient cosine **−0.9583**. Thus the small weighted loss is accompanied by a nonzero gradient, about 4% of supervised norm on this batch, mostly opposing it in parameter space. The V3 original Laplacian checkpoint has weighted gradient ratio 0.07811. V4 true-wind λ=1.0 reaches ratio 0.22314 and has worse test MAE. The one-batch magnitude-matched Laplacian has ratio 0.08105; matching scalar loss did **not** match gradient exposure. Across V3 true-wind seed-11 horizons, gradient ratios are 0.1275 (1h), 0.0324 (3h), 0.0398 (6h), 0.0298 (12h), 0.0422 (24h); see source CSV for norms and cosines.

These observations show a usable but variable optimization signal. They do not isolate a causal atmospheric mechanism. The sign and magnitude of a one-batch parameter-gradient cosine cannot be extrapolated to all epochs, seeds or weather states. Saved V3 training histories supply per-epoch raw losses and active arcs, while the replay supplies a checkpoint-local gradient measurement.
