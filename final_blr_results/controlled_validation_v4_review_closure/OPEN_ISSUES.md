# Unresolved scientific and provenance issues

1. **Independent validation:** The 2025-09-23–2026-03-28 test period had already been inspected. The V4 six-hour sensitivity results are exploratory, even though the V4 controls and comparison family were frozen before the new score. No untouched later Bengaluru period, isolated new station or external city was evaluated.
2. **Operational availability:** Archive weather has source timestamps but no verified publication time. Station PM2.5 readings have timestamps but no verified ingestion time. The repaired pollutant lag ensures timestamp causality, not a guaranteed operational issue-time SLA.
3. **Spatial support:** The 15-station graph has only 11 stations with scored test targets. Thousands of station-hours cannot substitute for independent geographic sites, road measurements or external-city replication.
4. **Regularizer specificity:** True wind beats reversed and fully shuffled masks at six hours, but fixed north and the one-batch magnitude-matched Laplacian have lower ensemble MAE. Five-seed signs vary for reversed and constant controls. Mask density and gradient exposure differ. Physical transport specificity is unresolved.
5. **Physics:** The hinge is a normalized concentration-ordering heuristic; no atmospheric control volumes, emissions, diffusion, boundary fluxes or mass-conservation equation enter it. A lower squared ordering magnitude is not proof of atmospheric correctness.
6. **Training selection:** The shared LR was selected from a seed-11 unregularized model using validation. This is matched optimization but gives the λ=0 procedure the selection opportunity. Baseline architectures use separate validation-selected LRs and parameter counts.
7. **Weather-edge scaling:** Adaptive-graph wind-along inputs denormalize the V3 weather channel with a historical v2j scaler (wind SD 1.423970 versus V2m 1.458159). The corrected GINEConv–GRU backbone does not use this derived edge tensor, but the adaptive graph does; see the implementation register.
8. **Uncertainty methods:** Moving-block bootstrap intervals and nonoverlapping-block sign-flip p-values use different dependence constructions and sometimes disagree. The 26 seven-day test blocks are not independent cities or sites. No confirmatory test is claimed.
9. **Anchor-age strata:** Error by anchor age is descriptive and confounded by site, season and missingness. The high imputed-input fraction and test distribution shift limit broad robustness conclusions.
10. **Backups:** The V4 ZIP is a local verified research archive and includes selected checkpoints/predictions, but not V3 raw data, V3 checkpoints, invalidated checkpoint tensors or restart-only partials. The independent USB backup remains unverified.
