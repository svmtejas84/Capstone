# Genuine observation-anchor age and error

The audit reads `observed_mask` from the repaired V3 array and finds the latest genuinely observed PM2.5 row **strictly before** each issue index c=T−h, per station. It does not use `input_anchor` values to decide whether an observation exists, because that array may contain a training-mean fallback. Age is c minus the prior observed row in hours. `ANCHOR_AGE_AND_ERROR.csv` reports prespecified 1, 2–3, 4–6, 7–12, 13–24, >24 and none buckets for each horizon, using V3 unregularized ensemble absolute error on matched observed test targets. `ANCHOR_AGE_PER_STATION.csv` reports per-site counts, fallback count, median and p90 age. These are descriptive, not independent temporal or spatial replicates.

At 6h, 13,340 of 14,583 scored pairs (91.48%) had a genuinely observed anchor exactly one hour before issue. Another 42 pairs (0.288%) had no prior observed PM2.5 anchor and used the train-only mean fallback. The remaining 1,201 pairs had older observed anchors; 309 exceeded 24 hours. The 6h unregularized MAE was 14.829 µg/m³ in the 1h bucket, 25.178 for 2–3h, 24.589 for 4–6h, 30.937 for 7–12h, 19.324 for 13–24h, 16.657 for >24h, and 16.348 for no prior anchor. Irregular patterns reflect different sites and periods; no age-effect causal estimate is identified.

Across horizons, one-hour anchors decline from 95.37% (1h forecast) to 84.42% (24h forecast). At 24h, 149 scored pairs have no prior observed anchor. This scored-target distribution must not be confused with the much higher fraction of **imputed historical pollutant input slots**: the V3 all-station twelve-hour windows had approximately 84.9% imputed PM2.5 slots in six-hour training and 76.7% in six-hour test (`metrics/data_coverage_shift.csv`). A sparse observed-input tensor can coexist with a recently observed residual anchor on the subset of hours that have scored targets. The slot mask is `input_observed_mask` in the repaired array, corresponding to source observation status at feature row c−1.
