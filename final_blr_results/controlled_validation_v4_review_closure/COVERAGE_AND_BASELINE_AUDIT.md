# Coverage denominator and full baseline inventory

The complete method register has exactly 16 methods × five horizons = 80 deterministic/ensemble rows, independently recomputed from saved per-pair NPZ predictions. Five baselines are deterministic and have zero trained seeds: persistence, same-hour-yesterday, station training mean, hour/day climatology and weather ridge. Six other baselines have five validation-selected trained seeds: GRU-only, vanilla GCN, ST-GRU-GCN, adaptive graph, MLP and TCN. The five matched GINEConv–GRU conditions are λ=0, true-wind, within-month shuffled wind, +90° rotated wind and Laplacian, each with five seeds. Five-seed ensembles are arithmetic means of saved per-seed predictions before metric calculation. The full MAE/RMSE/R² and exact sample counts are in `COMPLETE_MODEL_AND_BASELINE_REGISTER.csv` and `tables/COMPLETE_FIVE_HORIZON_EVALUATION.csv/.tex`.

The exposed test window spans 4,488 UTC hours, or 187 calendar days, and the model graph has 15 stations. Its full-window denominator is therefore 4,488×15 = 67,320 possible station-hours. The 14,583 observed target pairs give **21.662210%** coverage (`COVERAGE_AND_SHIFT.csv`). Eleven stations have scored observations; four have none. An earlier presentation used only 11 scored stations and 177 days containing at least one scored observation: 14,583/(11×177×24)=**31.2083%**. Both arithmetic ratios are valid under their stated denominators, but the 31.208% value does not measure full-window, full-graph coverage. Per-station test counts from the saved mask are: 5547=0, 5548=0, 5607=1620, 6973=302, 6974=8, 6975=0, 6983=1901, 6984=1877, 2498781=0, 3409385=2113, 3409388=2243, 3409393=2207, 6119271=845, 6146655=212, 6206921=1255. The count sums to 14,583.

The same full-graph/full-calendar denominator convention gives train coverage 32,638/(30,624×15)=7.105103% and validation coverage 7,203/(1,992×15)=24.106426%. Those are observed-target coverage figures, while model training eligibility also requires issue-hour input status and yields fewer scored training/validation pairs per horizon. The test score uses all 14,583 observed target pairs.

The six-hour training input-slot imputation fraction was 84.8949%; test input-slot fraction was 76.7190%. These are all-station 12-hour window fractions for h6, not scored-target missingness. Validation observed PM2.5 mean was 24.970 µg/m³ (n=7,203); test mean was 44.426 µg/m³ (n=14,583), indicating a substantial observed-label distribution shift. Other horizon-specific window imputation fractions are in the source CSV; do not generalize the h6 percentages across horizons.
