# Controlled V4 scientific review-closure verdict

Status: **complete exploratory saved-evidence audit and bounded six-hour sensitivity campaign**. The original V3 and earlier research files remain frozen. No independent Bengaluru period, external city, roadside labels or certified operational ingestion logs were available. The historical test interval had been repeatedly examined. The 30 new V4 fits were selected on validation only; all 36 new seed/ensemble prediction arrays were then scored once on the exposed test after a pushed protocol and checkpoint-verification gate. Four pre-amendment full-shuffle attempts were invalidated before scoring and remain preserved as failure history.

| Question | Classification | Numerical evidence and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Historical benchmark as forecast-origin-safe performance | **NOT SUPPORTED** | Historical centered weather uses a c+30-minute record after issue c, and same-hour floored pollutant inputs can include later readings. Old and V3 target arrays/masks match, and a checked h1 join matched all 14,583 pairs. Historical ST-PIGNN ensemble MAEs 8.026/12.342/14.706/15.509/13.902 versus repaired V3 11.161/13.624/15.366/15.422/14.597 across 1/3/6/12/24h; differences cannot be attributed solely to leakage because training and selection also differ. Historical figures are superseded for forecast-origin-safe claims. |

| Graph architecture advantage on the exposed temporal population | **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED** | The unregularized GINEConv–GRU ensemble has lower MAE than GRU-only at 1,6,12,24h and lower than TCN/MLP at several horizons, but differences are small, model sizes and baseline-selected LRs differ, and most 15-family adjusted contrasts are not below 0.05. See `GRAPH_ARCHITECTURE_COMPARISON.md`. Graph necessity and independent generalization are not established. |
| Directional regularization versus λ=0 | **INCONCLUSIVE** | At 6h true minus zero = −0.128652 µg/m³, moving-block 95% CI [−0.404159,+0.091673]. It worsens 1h and 24h ensemble MAE. All five horizons and seeds are retained. |
| Actual wind direction versus false-direction controls | **MIXED** | At 6h true λ=0.06 beats reverse by 0.046 and corrected full shuffle by 0.170 µg/m³ in ensemble MAE, but constant north is 0.094 better than true; only 2/5 seeds favor true versus reverse and 1/5 versus constant north. Historical within-month shuffle, +90° rotation and original Laplacian contrasts are in the frozen 20-row register. A unique physical-direction benefit is not established. |
| Directional penalty versus magnitude-matched Laplacian | **NOT SUPPORTED as superior** | One-batch raw-loss matched Laplacian coefficient 0.02184366 produced six-hour ensemble MAE 15.226 versus true wind 15.366. True minus matched Laplacian +0.139459, exploratory block CI [+0.073342,+0.202408]. Matching one raw loss does not match gradients: replayed weighted gradient ratios are 0.08105 Laplacian versus 0.03981 true. |
| Stronger true-wind penalty | **NOT SUPPORTED** | Six-hour ensemble MAE rises from 15.366 at λ=0.06 to 15.667 at 0.3 and 16.502 at 1.0; all five seeds favor λ=0.06 against each stronger setting. Larger λ lowers mean squared positive ordering difference but worsens test MAE. No additional strengths were searched. |
| Penalty optimization exposure | **SUPPORTED as nonzero but limited** | V3 h6 true-wind training selected 185,890 active arc-hours over 13,382 target-hours; no zero-active batches. A checkpoint-local first-batch replay gives raw penalty 0.145391, weighted penalty 0.008723, weighted gradient 0.117736 versus supervised gradient 2.957581 (ratio 3.98%). | Directional ordering violation frequency improvement | **NOT SUPPORTED** | On 5,817 common observed-endpoint arcs at h6, true wind 24.9785% versus λ=0 24.6003% at threshold 0.05 normalized. No independent robustness test. |
| Independent temporal, geographic, roadside or second-city generalization | **NOT EVALUATED** | Historical temporal test was exposed; no new station-isolated fold, independent period, road-level labels or London experiment in V4. |

## Evidence interpretation

V4 reinforces that the existing directional hinge has an optimization effect: it changes violation *magnitude*, its weighted gradient is nonzero, and stronger λ changes predictions. It does **not** establish a specifically correct atmospheric mechanism. True wind beats some false-direction controls, while fixed north and the magnitude-matched generic smoother have lower six-hour ensemble MAE. The six-hour improvement versus no regularization remains inconclusive. The result is sensitive to method definition, optimization exposure, seed, and dependence analysis. The entire test period is exploratory.
