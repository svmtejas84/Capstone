# v2m frozen baseline protocol

Frozen before MLP/TCN training, 2026-11-06. This is an exploratory post-hoc experiment on an exposed Bengaluru test set.

- Data: original 15 stations, 0–500 µg/m³ observed targets, original train/validation/test splits and gaps, 12-hour history, 18 channels (13 base, lag-is-imputed, four calendar), train-only normalization, same-space log1p, residual over last observed concentration at or before cutoff. Only weather input changes: at each hour c, use the latest corrected UTC half-hour weather observation timestamped at or before c (normally c−30 minutes). The historical centered v2j inputs and outputs remain preserved. Retrospective Archive weather is used as a feature whose timestamp precedes c; operational forecast availability is not established.
- Horizons: 1, 3, 6, 12, 24 hours. Seeds: 11, 23, 37, 51, 79. No test-pair exclusions. Test targets and masks must match v2j exactly.
- Models: station-wise MLP 216→64→64→1 with ReLU; station-wise causal TCN, 18→64 and 64→64 Conv1D (kernel 3, dilation 1 then 2), ReLU, flattened 12-position readout 768→1. No graph or physics in either.
- Training: AdamW; squared error in standardized log1p residual reconstruction space; gradient norm clip 0.3; width 64; batch 512; at most 30 epochs; early stopping patience 5; weight decay 0.01. LR candidates 0.0001, 0.0003, 0.001, 0.003. Select LR by seed-11 validation MAE per architecture/horizon, then train final five seeds at that LR, including seed 11 as a fresh final fit. Select checkpoint per seed by validation MAE. No test-based selection.
- Evaluation: all observed test pairs, including pairs with imputed lag inputs. Report each seed, mean individual-seed metrics, and five-seed mean-prediction ensemble. Pair by exact UTC target timestamp and station ID. Bootstrap matched UTC days with 2,000 resamples, seed 4303; calculate observation-weighted paired MAE difference and percentile 95% CI. Primary family: ST-PIGNN minus MLP and ST-PIGNN minus TCN at all five horizons, ten comparisons, Holm correction. Historical v2j controls are descriptive cross-protocol comparisons because weather information sets differ. Do not describe them as identical-input ablations or independent confirmation.
- Supervision: V100 CUDA only for training; single-instance `scripts/run_supervised.sh`; per-cell atomic files and completion markers; no silent skipped seeds. All v2m artifacts under `final_blr_results/baselines/v2m/`.
- Backup: verified pre-training local ZIP, external copy pending under explicit supervisor exception. Supplementary ZIP required after training.
