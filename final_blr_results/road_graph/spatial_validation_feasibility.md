# Leakage-free spatial validation feasibility

The corrected v2j W6 LOSO protocol scores 11 of 15 monitored stations; four folds lack observed test targets and remain unscored (`results/loso_unscored_folds_v2j.csv`, `results/loso_summary_v2j.csv`). Saved LOSO predictions can re-evaluate those station locations on the **station graph**. No compatible per-fold trained v2j checkpoint was saved for a road-graph forward pass. The remaining mapped station nodes could be considered future held-out road-node targets only where observed station PM2.5 and a complete, aligned hourly road-node feature sequence exist. That condition is not currently met for a full-road graph evaluation.

For a valid future held-out station, exclude its PM2.5 and other pollutant history from input features, imputation, station/train normalization, graph-derived station priors, calibration and model selection. Keep its true observations only for final scoring on the fixed test times. Weather and static road attributes may remain if provenance and time alignment are verified. A training-station concentration copied to its road node is leakage; an imputed road concentration is not an accuracy target. Mapping one monitor to a road node also introduces spatial representativeness error.

Existing LOSO predictions support limited station-graph transfer analysis, but cannot demonstrate road-node transfer, full-graph inference, or street-level accuracy. A new approved experiment would need fixed graph construction, held-out mappings, complete corrected node-hour inputs, compatible checkpoints, matched evaluation pairs, and a spatial uncertainty plan. No LOSO retraining was initiated in v2k.
