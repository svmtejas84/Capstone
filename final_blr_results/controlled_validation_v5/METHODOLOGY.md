# V5 numerical-method fact sheet

## Evaluation unit and information cutoff

For observed station target (y_{T,s}) at UTC target hour (T), a nominal (h)-hour forecast is issued at (c=T-h). The V3 repaired array supplies 12 feature rows (c-11,ldots,c); each pollutant feature displayed at row (c) originates at source row at most (c-1). Past archived weather stamped (c-30) minutes and issue-row wind direction are used. Actual weather-release and station-ingestion delays are unmeasured. The observed test target is never a feature. V5 preserves the 2022-01-01–2025-06-29 training interval, 2025-07-01–2025-09-21 validation interval and previously exposed 2025-09-23–2026-03-28 test interval. There are 14,583 observed test station-hour pairs per horizon. Source and split details: V3 `DATA_ELIGIBILITY.md`, `configs/data_v2.yaml`, V5 `FORECAST_LEAD_AND_INFORMATION_PARITY.md`.

## Forecasting backbone and objective

All learned V5 conditions use the corrected 15-station GINEConv–GRU residual forecaster: two GINEConv spatial layers, one GRU temporal layer, hidden width 64 and dropout 0.1. The original graph has 60 directed four-nearest-neighbor arcs and a one-dimensional UTM edge-distance attribute in metres. The 18-channel input comprises 13 base environmental channels, one delayed-PM2.5 missingness flag and four cyclic calendar channels. L1 alone adds three daily-lag channels, bringing its parameter count to 43,585 instead of 43,393. L2 changes only temporal width to 36, with 43,393 parameters. Input and model definitions: `COMPLETE_IMPLEMENTATION_REGISTER.md`, `gnn/model.py`, `src/stpignn/models_v2.py`, V5 `scripts/train_graph_lag.py`.

Let $z_{T,s}$ be the model's normalized-log1p PM2.5 output after adding the strictly prior residual anchor in log space, and let $\mu,\sigma$ be train-only log1p mean/SD. The supervised objective is the masked mean of $[z_{T,s}-(\log(1+y_{T,s})-\mu)/\sigma]^2$ over observed eligible training targets. At scoring, $\hat y_{T,s}=\exp(\mu+\sigma z_{T,s})-1$, reported in µg/m³. Train/validation additionally require the issue-row delayed observation indicator, whereas exposed-test scoring includes every observed target; this inherited selection difference is reported rather than hidden. Batch 512, AdamW learning rate from the historical validation-selected unregularized seed-11 grid, weight decay .01, gradient clipping .3, 30-epoch cap, best validation MAE checkpoint only from epoch 10 onward, patience five. Same five fixed seeds in every new condition.

## Graph intervention

G0 reuses the frozen V3 unregularized original graph and checkpoints. G1 explicitly passes 15 self-loop edges of zero length and no interstation edge to the same network. G2 swaps destinations of directed edges under NumPy seed 5505 subject to no duplicates or self loops; every node retains its original in-degree and out-degree, and every resulting edge length is recomputed from its actual endpoint UTM coordinates. The fixed realization retains 20 original arcs and replaces 40 of 60. G0, G1 and G2 have identical node features, loss, parameter count, seed initialization, learning rate, optimizer and scoring population. G1/G2 training remains unregularized. A single rewired realization is evidence about that control, not a topology distribution.

## Daily-lag and 36-hour interventions

L1 has 12 temporal rows; its final row has three new channels. The value is the observed $y_{T-24,s}$ only if $T-24<c$ and it was genuinely observed. Otherwise it is the strictly prior anchor at $T-24$, matching the frozen same-hour-yesterday baseline; if no prior observation exists, that anchor is the training-station mean. The second channel indicates genuine T−24 observation. The third is $\min(168,c-t_{\rm source})/168$, with 1 for no actual prior source. The value channel uses the same train-only log1p normalization as the target. At 24 h, T−24 equals issue, so the actual issue-hour observation is excluded: genuine daily-lag availability is zero by design. L2 uses rows $c-35,\ldots,c$, left-padding only at the dataset start with row zero so all target masks remain identical. L1's extra 192 parameters and L2's triple temporal execution are explicit capacity/compute differences. A six-hour secondary cross-factor trains L1 with G1/G2 to test whether a graph effect persists when daily-lag input is held fixed.

## Regularizer-family grid

For a predicted physical concentration $\hat y$, training standard deviation $s_p$ in µg/m³, edge $i\to j$ of length $d_{ij}$ metres, and issue-hour mask $m_{ij}$, the implemented directional penalty is

\[
R_D=\frac{\sum_{b,i\to j}m_{b,ij}\left[\max\left(0,\frac{\hat y_{b,j}-\hat y_{b,i}}{s_p}\,\operatorname{sigmoid}(d_{ij}/1000)\right)\right]^2}{\max(1,\sum_{b,i\to j}m_{b,ij})}.
\]

The training objective is supervised MSE plus (\lambda_eR_D), with (\lambda_e=\lambda_{\max}\min(1,e/5)). The actual-wind mask uses wind-FROM compass bearing plus 180° and source-to-destination UTM bearing within ±37.5°. A finite bearing can activate arcs at zero wind speed; NaN wind direction activates none. Monthly shuffle seed 4303, training-calendar full shuffle seed 4404, 180° reverse, constant 0° north transport, and Laplacian are the frozen controls. Predictive weather features are never randomized. The Laplacian uses mean squared normalized difference on all directed arcs, without the directional geometric multiplier; its raw scale differs. Each nonzero family has λ∈{.06,.3,1.0}, with five-seed mean validation MAE selecting one coefficient per family before new-model test scoring. The V4 one-batch raw-magnitude-matched Laplacian coefficient .02184366393360663 is retained as a separate historical reference, not substituted into the V5 grid. Penalty and gradient exposure are measured and reported; same λ is not treated as equal effective regularization. The directional hinge is a concentration-ordering heuristic, not atmospheric mass conservation.

## Metrics and uncertainty

For each condition and horizon, report MAE, RMSE, $R^2$, observed target count, five individual seeds and the arithmetic mean of five seed predictions. Every paired contrast is evaluated on exactly aligned target hour, station index and true PM2.5. Negative $\Delta\mathrm{MAE}=\mathrm{MAE}_{\rm first}-\mathrm{MAE}_{\rm second}$ favors the first model. CIs use 2,000 seven-day moving-block bootstrap draws. P-values use 100,000 seven-day nonoverlapping-block sign flips. Fixed deterministic seeds are in V5 `scripts/analyze_v5.py`; Holm correction is separate for ten graph, ten temporal, two secondary lag-matched graph, and six selected-family comparisons. Earlier V3 20-member and V4 six-member families remain separate. Station-hours, seeds and temporal blocks are not independent cities or sites. Every test inference is exploratory due prior exposure.
