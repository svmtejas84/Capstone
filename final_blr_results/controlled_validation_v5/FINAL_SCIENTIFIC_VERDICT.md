## RQ1 — Does original station-graph topology improve forecasting?

**Verdict: PARTIALLY SUPPORTED for interstation edges; general topology necessity INCONCLUSIVE.** The original unregularized 60-arc graph has lower ensemble MAE than the 15-self-loop control at every horizon, but the frozen degree-preserving rewiring is extremely close and beats the original at 3, 6 and 24 hours. This is a single rewiring realization, with geometric distances recomputed for all new arcs. Both controls use the same 43,393-parameter GINEConv–GRU backbone and training rules.

| Horizon | Original graph | Self loops | Rewired | Units |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1 h | 10.896 | 10.913 | 10.916 | MAE, µg/m³ |
| 3 h | 13.668 | 13.784 | 13.656 | MAE, µg/m³ |
| 6 h | 15.494 | 15.692 | 15.477 | MAE, µg/m³ |
| 12 h | 15.378 | 16.270 | 15.395 | MAE, µg/m³ |
| 24 h | 14.304 | 14.675 | 14.268 | MAE, µg/m³ |

The largest original-versus-self-loop ensemble difference is −0.891 µg/m³ at 12 hours (seven-day block 95% CI −1.108 to −0.683; five of five seeds favor original). At six hours, original minus self loops is −0.198 (CI −0.410 to 0.006); original minus rewired is **+0.018** (CI −0.011 to 0.040). At 24 hours, rewired is better by 0.0365, despite an adjusted exploratory p-value of 0.0104 in the ten-contrast graph family. The one-realization rewiring and exposed test limit any general claim. [SOURCE: `tables/T1_GRAPH_TOPOLOGY.csv`; `statistical_analysis/GRAPH_TOPOLOGY_CONTRASTS.csv`; `statistical_analysis/V5_SEED_LEVEL.csv`.]

The secondary six-hour lag-matched check holds the added daily-lag input fixed. The original graph scores 14.105, self loops 14.587 and rewired 14.154 µg/m³. Original minus self loops is −0.482 (CI −0.641 to −0.329; five of five seeds), and original minus rewired is −0.0485 (CI −0.0781 to −0.0195; four of five seeds). This supports a modest graph effect **for this six-hour lag-matched configuration**, not a universal necessity claim. [SOURCE: `tables/T2_LAG_INFORMATION.csv`; `statistical_analysis/LAG_MATCHED_GRAPH_CONTRASTS.csv`.]

## RQ2 — Does causal daily-lag information change the comparison with temporal baselines?

**Verdict: PARTIALLY SUPPORTED.** Adding the fixed three-channel daily-lag value/availability/age input to the last row of the 12-hour GINEConv–GRU improves ensemble MAE over the original 12-hour input at all five horizons, with all five seed-level paired differences favoring the augmented model at each horizon. The paired L0-minus-L1 MAE differences are +0.313, +0.854, +1.389, +0.441 and +0.389 µg/m³ at 1, 3, 6, 12 and 24 hours, respectively; all five corresponding seven-day block CIs have positive lower bounds. The 36-hour lookback also helps at several horizons, but it triples temporal computation; the daily-lag model adds 192 parameters. These are information/capacity changes, not pure evidence that the graph became better. [SOURCE: `tables/T2_LAG_INFORMATION.csv`; `statistical_analysis/LAG_INFORMATION_CONTRASTS.csv`; `statistical_analysis/V5_SEED_LEVEL.csv`.]

At six hours, the augmented model MAE is 14.105 versus 14.130 for the saved same-hour-yesterday baseline. Their descriptive paired difference is −0.025 µg/m³, with a 95% block interval of −0.745 to +0.871: **no established advantage over this simple baseline**. At 12 hours the simple baseline remains better (14.130 versus 14.938). At 24 hours, the actual `T−24` reading equals issue time and is barred by the strict-prior rule, so there are zero genuine daily-lag inputs; the 13.915 versus 14.972 comparison reflects fallback/age channels and model learning, not an observed previous-day reading. The same-hour-yesterday h24 control equals strict-prior persistence. [SOURCE: `statistical_analysis/DAILY_LAG_SIMPLE_BASELINE.csv`; `LAG24_ELIGIBILITY.csv`; `FORECAST_LEAD_AND_INFORMATION_PARITY.md`.]

## RQ3 — Does actual wind outperform fairly selected alternative penalties?

**Verdict: INCONCLUSIVE for physical specificity.** The 18-row strength grid and validation-only five-seed mean rule selected maximum λ values: actual wind 0.06; within-month shuffle, full shuffle, reverse wind and Laplacian 1.0; constant north 0.3. All selected checkpoints occurred at epoch ≥10 and therefore reached their five-epoch warmup maximum. The selected-procedure six-hour exposed-test ensemble MAEs are 15.366 (actual wind), 15.494 (zero), 16.629 (monthly shuffle), 16.506 (full shuffle), 16.317 (reverse), 15.688 (north) and 16.332 (Laplacian) µg/m³. The selected actual-wind procedure beats each selected nonzero control by point estimate and block CI, but its advantage over zero is −0.129 (CI −0.419 to +0.095; three of five seeds) and is inconclusive. [SOURCE: `metrics/FAIRNESS_GRID_VALIDATION.csv`; `metrics/FAIRNESS_VALIDATION_SELECTION.json`; `tables/T3_REGULARIZER_GRID.csv`; `statistical_analysis/FAIRNESS_SELECTED_CONTRASTS.csv`.]

The selected controls carry **unequal optimization exposure**. On the first fixed training batch at their selected checkpoints, the mean ratio of weighted-penalty to supervised gradient norms is 0.0359 for actual wind λ=.06, versus 0.1174 north λ=.3, 0.1907 monthly shuffle λ=1, 0.2645 full shuffle λ=1, 0.3076 reverse λ=1 and 0.4264 Laplacian λ=1. Directional masks cover about 15–24.5% of arcs depending on control, while Laplacian covers all arcs. The validation/test shift is substantial: observed PM2.5 mean rises from 24.970 to 44.426 µg/m³. At common λ=.06, constant north scores 15.272, better than actual wind 15.366. The validation-selected false-wind procedures' poor test outcomes therefore do not isolate correctness of wind direction. Neither a concentration-ordering hinge nor lower ordering-difference magnitude demonstrates mass conservation. [SOURCE: `metrics/FAIRNESS_GRADIENT_REPLAY.csv`; `data_manifests/COVERAGE_AND_SHIFT.csv`; `PENALTY_DIAGNOSTICS_COMPLETE.csv`.]

## RQ4 — Are effects consistent across seeds, horizons and optimization?

**Verdict: MIXED.** The daily-lag L1 input improves the original 12-hour model in five of five seeds at every horizon. Original graph versus self loops favors the original in 4/5, 4/5, 5/5, 5/5 and 5/5 seeds across increasing horizons, whereas original graph versus rewired favors it in only 3/5, 3/5, 1/5, 4/5 and 2/5. Selected actual wind beats selected false-wind and Laplacian controls in 4/5 or 5/5 seeds at six hours, but beats zero in only 3/5. These counts are descriptive; seeds are not independent sites or cities. [SOURCE: `statistical_analysis/V5_SEED_LEVEL.csv`.]

Training LR, initialization rule, target masks, graph and backbone are matched within each intended control comparison, with five fixed seeds. The shared LR was inherited from a V3 seed-11 **unregularized** validation choice; it may favor that procedure. Penalty families have different active arcs and gradient magnitudes, even when coefficients are on the same grid. The V5 family-specific validation selection was frozen and correctly applied, but it does not resolve causal attribution to actual wind direction. Six new runs hit the 30-epoch cap. [SOURCE: `TRAINING_SELECTION_REGISTER.csv`; `metrics/training_summary.json`; `metrics/FAIRNESS_GRADIENT_REPLAY.csv`; `METHODOLOGY.md`.]
