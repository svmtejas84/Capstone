# Saved V3/V4 contrast and penalty evidence

`COMPLETE_CONTRAST_REGISTER.csv` retains the **20-member originally frozen V3 family** and the **six later V4 exploratory contrasts** as separate families. `SEED_LEVEL_COMPARISONS.csv` preserves **130** paired seed rows. Their block-bootstrap confidence intervals, 100,000-draw seven-day block sign-flip p-values and family-specific Holm adjustments come from the saved V3/V4 artifacts; V5 does not retroactively join their multiplicity families. Source columns remain in the register, including `holm_20_p` and `holm_6_p`.

`PENALTY_DIAGNOSTICS_COMPLETE.csv` recomputes a **common actual-wind post-hoc ordering diagnostic** for five saved V3 regularization conditions at each horizon and six V4 six-hour conditions, **31 rows**. An eligible arc requires (i) an actual-wind source-to-destination bearing within ±37.5° at forecast issue and (ii) both endpoint predictions having scored observed targets at the same target hour. The threshold is **0.05 normalized physical-target units**, approximately **1.149687 µg/m³**. Frequency is the fraction of eligible arcs with destination-minus-source normalized prediction greater than 0.05. Squared positive ordering difference is averaged over **all eligible arcs**, including zero differences. This is separate from the training penalty, which can act on unobserved station predictions and uses its own edge weighting.

At six hours, both models had **5,817 eligible arcs**. True-wind regularization had **24.9785%** violations and mean squared positive difference **0.238266**. No regularization had **24.6003%** violations and mean squared positive difference **0.293381**. Thus the true-wind model lowered *magnitude* on this diagnostic while having a slightly higher *violation frequency*. Neither metric establishes physical conservation or atmospheric correctness. The raw/weighted penalty and gradient-ratio columns refer only to the **seed-11 selected-checkpoint, first-train-batch replay** where V4 measured them; they are marked `NOT MEASURED` elsewhere. They must not be interpreted as exposed-test penalty values or inferred from the post-hoc diagnostic. V5 plans additional checkpoint-local replay for the complete fairness grid, without optimizer steps.
