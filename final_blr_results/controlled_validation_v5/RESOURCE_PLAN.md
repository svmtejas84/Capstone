# V5 resource plan and measured pilot

Hardware checked 2026-11-15: NVIDIA Tesla V100-PCIE-32GB, 32,768 MiB total, 32,488 MiB free before V5; host filesystem 95 GiB free. The four one-batch V5 smoke passes used shapes B×12×15×18, B×12×15×21, and B×36×15×18 and finite gradients. Parameter counts were 43,393 for graph/36-hour conditions and 43,585 for the lag-24 augmented model. During the graph stage `nvidia-smi` showed 1,990 MiB used and 47% GPU utilization. V3's measured one-batch peak was 1.21 GiB allocated; this remains a historical comparison, not a V5 peak-allocation measurement.

Frozen maximum: **160 new fits** after amendment 01: 50 graph, 50 temporal, 10 six-hour lag-matched graph, 50 regularizer grid. The first completed self-loop h1 seed-11 fit used **18.49 recorded fit seconds**; the first rewired h1 seed-11 fit used **22.32 s**. A naïve 20–30 s/cell projection is 0.9–1.3 recorded GPU fit hours, with 36-hour windows expected to take longer. The hard stop is **four recorded V100 fit-hours** and **four GiB** of new selected/restart checkpoints. The training directory was 17 MiB after 17 cells. Stage jobs are sequential; no two training workers should share the V100.

Execution is through `scripts/run_supervised.sh` and V5 `scripts/run_stage.sh`. Each cell writes atomic `partial.pt` per epoch, `best.pt`, epoch history and `COMPLETE.json`. A worker rerun skips and verifies completed cells. The supervisor stores its command, process ID, attempts and completion marker under V5 `logs/<stage>/`. Check status with elevated `bash scripts/run_supervised.sh status final_blr_results/controlled_validation_v5/logs/graph` (similarly `lag`, `cross`, `fairness`). The sandbox-only process namespace can report STOPPED while the elevated V100 worker is still RUNNING; use the elevated command for process decisions. No `tmux` is used.

If a stage fails, inspect its `attempts.log` and latest `attempt_*.log`, then resume only after diagnosing the failure; existing `COMPLETE.json` cells are preserved. If budget is exhausted, stop the lowest-priority unfinished workstream and report it NOT RUN or INCOMPLETE. Do not score its test results as though its full frozen matrix completed.
