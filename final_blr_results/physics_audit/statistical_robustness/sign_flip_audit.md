# v2k sign-flip and Holm audit

The v2k script flips **one sign per UTC-day sum** of paired absolute-error differences, not one sign per station. The statistic remains observation-weighted because the denominator is the fixed total pair count. For each horizon the RNG first generates the 2,000 bootstrap samples and then 2,000 day-level ±1 matrices. Replaying that exact order and seeds 4406, 4613, 4920, 5527, 6734 reproduces the five physics-versus-λ=0 p values: 0.041979, 0.000500, 0.000500, 0.000500 and 0.079460. The original 35-test Holm adjusted values are 0.445277, 0.017491, 0.017491, 0.017491 and 0.635682.

This implementation respects within-day station grouping. It does **not** establish that day-level effects are symmetrically distributed or sign-exchangeable under the null. Day effects can be skewed by pollution episodes, vary by month, and be serially correlated. The seven-day moving-block bootstrap tests one aspect of dependence but does not validate sign-flip null assumptions. Monte Carlo p values have minimum 1/2001; the identical 0.000500 values reflect resolution, not equal underlying strength. All adjusted results remain exploratory and post-hoc.
