# Open scientific and asset issues

- Corrected main-benchmark checkpoint identities do not support attribution coverage for every historical model cell; displayed Captum variants are limited to the compatible saved one-hour and six-hour station-isolated variants.
- The ST-PIGNN versus ridge station-isolation comparison uses different output reconstruction anchors; the population is matched, but reconstruction is not fully identical.
- Bengaluru has only 11 scored spatial holdout stations; four sites lack test targets. Station bootstrap intervals are wide and do not establish broad spatial transfer.
- Test observations were repeatedly examined across historical model variants; all recovered benchmark plots remain exploratory.
- Historical centered-weather preprocessing uses a record after nominal forecast cutoff; cutoff-safe MLP/TCN results are a different protocol, not a strict architecture comparison.
- Full citywide road estimates have no independent roadside accuracy validation and the decoder underperformed simple controls in the held-out evaluation.
- A corrected post-hoc station-space wind-cone monotonicity diagnostic is available and tabulated in T03b. It covers only the 15-station graph and selected endpoint predictions; it is not a road-network diagnostic, physical-compliance test, or conservation result. Historical original-manuscript PVR remains unverified.
- Routing perturbations are deterministic stress scenarios, not calibrated uncertainty. Modeled-dose differences are negligible and do not measure real commuter exposure.
- Weather sources and legacy elevation features vary by pipeline; no CAMS input is claimed for the corrected station arrays or decoder.
- LaTeX engine was unavailable in the environment; snippets are statically checked, not compiled.
