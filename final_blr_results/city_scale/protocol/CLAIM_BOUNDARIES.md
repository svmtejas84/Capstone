# v2n claim boundaries

This is an exploratory closure on an exposed Bengaluru test set. Corrected v2j ST-PIGNN and v2m MLP/TCN use different weather aggregation protocols; a cross-round difference cannot isolate architecture. The 15-station graph supports monitored-station forecasting; the 154,902-node road graph supports topology engineering. They are not interchangeable validation populations.

Allowed statements require exact source artifacts: historical matched v2j neural comparisons, v2l mixed weak-physics effects, v2m cross-protocol MLP/TCN contrasts, saved W6 station holdouts, and v2k structural graph integrity. Do not state independent replication, atmospheric conservation, physically specific wind transport, road-node accuracy, exposure benefit, or citywide routing guarantees. Captum input attribution is baseline-relative model sensitivity, not causal intervention or edge attribution. Failed or missing experiments remain explicit in the final matrix.
