Preserve unscored station folds explicitly.

For decoder training, support-station forecasts must be **cross-fitted**: each prediction is made by a model whose fitting excludes that prediction's station's target and the outer held-out station's targets. Split training time chronologically into two predetermined blocks with ≥24-hour gap; train inner models on eligible earlier data only, predict later training block, and never use an in-sample forecast as a decoder training feature. Fold-specific scalers and imputers use only fitting information. Do not combine support predictions from folds whose fitting includes the outer held-out station. If complete aligned cross-fitted arrays cannot be produced within a measured budget, report NOT RUN and stop the learned decoder rather than weaken isolation.
