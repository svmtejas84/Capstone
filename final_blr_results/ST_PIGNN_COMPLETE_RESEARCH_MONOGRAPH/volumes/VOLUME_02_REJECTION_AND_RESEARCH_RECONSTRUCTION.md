## Editorial reconstruction and evidentiary boundary

Thus the issue matrices below are topic-level reconciliation, not complete reviewer quotations or per-reviewer attribution. In particular, the project stopped equating city graph construction with road-level prediction accuracy, and stopped equating a directional hinge with conservation. The primary review source is needed before declaring every criticism reconciled. ### Bengaluru research history and authority map

The original production notebook is a historical road-partition GINE–GRU pipeline; its full-city construction did not establish a valid forecast information set. Corrected v2j moved station forecasting to a 15-monitor graph with a repaired 18-channel input and chronological evaluation. Subsequent v2k–v2i audits examined graph integrity, data/model validity, baselines, LOSO, physics and robustness; those rounds contain protocol changes and repeated test exposure, so claims must preserve each round's label. v2l reused saved predictions to attribute the directional penalty; v2m completed 50 MLP/TCN cells under a distinct weather protocol. v2n consolidated city-scale and Captum evidence; v2o conducted retrospective spatial estimation; v2p implemented RGDR feasibility; v2q recovered the historical notebook and specified a decoder; v2r performed leakage-controlled h6 cross-fitting/LOSO, three full-city snapshots, and stopped RGDR before training on a frozen feasibility failure.

The detailed rows are in `EXPERIMENT_SOURCE_REGISTER.csv`. It contains UNVERIFIABLE/NOT RUN fields rather than inferred values where artifacts are missing. Never combine retrospective same-hour interpolation with forecast-origin results or historical notebook scores with corrected station evaluation.
