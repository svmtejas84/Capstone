# Routing validation supplement to frozen IEEE evidence package

This additive supplement references `final_blr_results/final_routing_closure/v2t/`; the existing evidence package remains unchanged elsewhere. It is evidence for later manuscript drafting, not manuscript prose.

**Historical implementation:** `matcher.gale_shapley.batch_match` is a commuter-proposing route-capacitated matcher. The audit found an unchecked fallback and no shared-road-edge representation. The separate historical offline allocator did track shared directed edges; its prior 1,000-request simulation admitted 444 and increased admitted-only modeled dose by 0.1211% and time by 0.1463%.

**Correction:** Additive `matcher.edge_capacity_da.allocate_shared_edges` preserves original commuter/route preference definitions and enforces both route and directed-edge quotas. It releases entire route bundles on displacement and returns `UNASSIGNED` if no feasible route remains. The API selects it by default; an environment switch retains the original control. Correctness suite 20/20; independent scan of 6,048 saved method-request rows found zero invalid paths and zero declared-edge-capacity violations.

**Frozen comparison:** 1,428-node central road snapshot, 12/24/48 requests, seeds 11/23/37, four concentration scenarios, six route/allocation methods, 36 batches. All 252 requests per scenario were admitted by every method; original/corrected paths matched in all 1,008 cases, max edge utilization 5.405%. On original concentrations, corrected mean batch-paired modeled-dose change versus shortest was −0.001207% and mean travel overhead +0.001324%; no practical exposure benefit was demonstrated. Uniform +25% concentration left all choices unchanged; spatial sine and support-distance perturbations changed some paths without changing admission or feasibility.

**Scientific boundary:** The concentration field was an unvalidated historical model snapshot, OD demand was synthetic, capacity constants and RMV/speed were assumed, and no road or personal exposure measurements exist. The algorithm's directed-edge quota feasibility is a software property under declared inputs. Classical stability, welfare optimality, real traffic feasibility, operational citywide runtime and exposure reduction remain unproved. Use the v2t claim decision and methodology before updating any manuscript claim or figure.
