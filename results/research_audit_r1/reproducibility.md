| Recovery | Existing checkpoints save weights and best epoch but lack optimizer, scheduler, AMP, RNG, batch progress and dataset fingerprint. **Regeneration classes.** P1–P3, W3, conformal and group-DRO metrics were recomputed from saved predictions by the R1 audit script. W2 and W7 have saved prediction files and source summarizers. W4's saved MAE/R² need retraining for independent verification because per-pair outputs are absent; draw membership and target populations are recomputable. P0's claimed paired baseline gain is not reproducible from available predictions. W8 is data-only recomputable from raw/static assets. The machine manifest lists all source paths and exact safe commands or identifies the missing metrics-only command. `REGENERABLE FROM CHECKPOINTS` is limited: older point model checkpoints have weights, but full training replay and exact interruption recovery are not available.

**Audit limits.** Numerical match to a saved prediction cache establishes arithmetic consistency, not unbiased model selection, physical interpretation or independent raw-data validity. The weather timezone concern and repeated test exposure require resolution before prospective external validation.
