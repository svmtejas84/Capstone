## London count method and limits

The small [LAQN station/species JSON](https://api.erg.ic.ac.uk/AirQuality/Information/MonitoringSiteSpecies/GroupName=London/Json) was queried on 2026-11-05. Its 254 site records contain 85 with `@SpeciesCode=PM25`. Filtering the PM25 measurement-start/finish metadata to at least 730 days inside **2022–2025** leaves 47 site codes. For those 47 only, 188 small [annual monitoring-report JSON](https://api.erg.ic.ac.uk/AirQuality/Annual/MonitoringReport/SiteCode=BX2/Year=2024/Json) requests (47×2022–2025) returned without HTTP errors. Twenty-five site-year PM25 capture values were absent or `-999`, mostly where the site was not yet operating; they were treated as zero for an **estimate**, not silently imputed as measured hours. Two or more years had positive annual PM25 capture at 45 sites. Multiplying the rounded annual capture percentage by 8,760/8,784 calendar hours gives at least 17,520 estimated hours at 35 sites. Exact hourly valid counts, method at each site/year, quality flags and station class were **not** verified. The 47-site metadata and capture summary is in `results/london_station_eligibility_v2i.csv`; its `instrument_method` column is explicitly `unverified`. No AURN/LAQN overlap was counted twice, because the numeric screen used LAQN alone. The wider [London government report](https://www.london.gov.uk/media/105046/download) reports over 150 reference-grade London sites across *all pollutants*, which cannot substitute for the PM2.5 eligibility count.

The [LAQN API endpoint list](https://api.erg.ic.ac.uk/airquality/help) documents hourly site-species data and a separate traffic-counter feed. Its [API documentation](https://api.erg.ic.ac.uk/AirQuality/Information/Documentation/pdf) says OGL v2 and includes an attribution example, while the [LAQN landing page](https://londonair.org.uk/LondonAir/API/) only says Open Government Licence. The [National Archives OGL terms](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) describe attributed redistribution for v3; do not treat LAEI's v3 label as automatically covering LAQN. [LAEI 2022](https://data.london.gov.uk/dataset/london-atmospheric-emissions-inventory-laei-2022-2lg5g) explicitly labels OGL v3 and gives 1-km annual PM2.5 grid emissions (tonnes/year), link-level major-road inventories and annual traffic flows/speeds. Temporal profiles, fleet-specific factors, non-road sources, units and uncertainty would need approval to construct hourly Q.

## Delhi source limits

The official [Delhi AQI site](https://aqi.delhi.gov.in/) and [station page](https://aqi.delhi.gov.in/stations) report the current 60-site network; the [CPCB/DPCC description](https://aqi.delhi.gov.in/about) identifies continuous monitoring sources. These are live status counts only. Neither a station's lead AQI pollutant nor a single day's report establishes historical hourly PM2.5 coverage. The [Delhi AQI reuse statement](https://aqi.delhi.gov.in/live) says GODL-India; [GODL text](https://data.gov.in/Godl) permits attributed copying, publication and derived works, while the [OGD catalog](https://www.data.gov.in/catalog/real-time-air-quality-index) identifies CPCB/NDSAP. A specific raw historical extract still needs its own terms, quality flags and instrument metadata checked.

The official [IITM SAFAR inventory announcement](https://www.tropmet.res.in/156-event_details) describes a released 400-m 2018 inventory. The primary [Sahu et al. 2023 dataset paper](https://essd.copernicus.org/articles/15/3183/2023/index.html) describes ~0.4-km sectoral PM2.5 grids for the 2020 base year in Gg/year. [Delhi Transport's road-safety appendix](https://transport.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/inline-files/east_appendix.pdf) includes sampled hourly traffic flow at individual locations; this is neither a full 2022–2026 road-network flow series nor a PM2.5 emission rate.

## Common meteorology and elevation

[ERA5 hourly single levels](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/reanalysis-era5-single-levels?tab=overview), DOI 10.24381/cds.adbb2d47, covers both cities on a 0.25° atmospheric grid and lists CC-BY licence. Actual site-to-grid mapping and sufficiently distinct weather series would still need checking. [USGS SRTM 1 Arc-Second Global](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/eros/science/usgs-eros-archive-digital-elevation-shuttle-radar-topography-mission-srtm-1), DOI 10.5066/F7PR7TFT, offers openly distributed ~30-m elevation globally, including both city areas; site sampling has not been performed.

**Gate conclusion:** London and Delhi have **unknown** eligible-station counts under the fixed two-year reference-grade hourly PM2.5 rule. The observed 35 London coverage candidates are not a verified eligible count. Neither city has a verified hourly PM2.5 Q. Both remain blocked from model training pending a station-level instrument/valid-hour audit and a separate, approved Q construction.
